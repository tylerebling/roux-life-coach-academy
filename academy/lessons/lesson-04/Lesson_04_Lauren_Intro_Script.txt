Welcome to Lesson Four: Values, Identity, and Personal Truth.

In this lesson, we will explore how to help a client recognize what matters beneath roles, expectations, and performance. You will learn to make values practical, widen identity beyond a single title, and translate personal truth into honest, responsible action.

The purpose is not to tell clients who they are. It is to create a conversation in which they can hear themselves more clearly, choose with courage, and remain accountable for what comes next.
