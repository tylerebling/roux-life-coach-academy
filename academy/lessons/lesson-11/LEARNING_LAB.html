REALISTIC COACHING SCENARIO

Tasha is dependable for everyone except herself

Tasha, age 39, manages work crises, family errands, and last-minute favors. She feels resentful and exhausted, yet fears that saying no will make her selfish and unlovable. The coach helps her identify capacity, choose a values-aligned limit, prepare for guilt and pushback, and follow through without punishing anyone.

Listen forObservable demands, actual capacity, body signals, inherited rules, feared consequences, values, relationship patterns, support, choice, and safety.
Do notCall others toxic, prescribe estrangement, make the decision, frame all discomfort as abuse, diagnose people-pleasing, or treat trauma and clinical symptoms.
TryIf guilt were allowed to be present without making the decision, what limit would protect your health and your integrity this week?
