Renee is a capable nonprofit executive who manages pressure well in public. At home and at work, she minimizes needs, prepares perfect answers, and withdraws when conversations become emotionally uncertain. Your role is to help her understand what the armor protects, assess trust and readiness, and choose a proportionate act of courage.

Listen forThe cue, feared outcome, protective strategy, short-term benefit, longer-term cost, trust evidence, and desired connection.
Do notPraise oversharing, diagnose avoidance, force disclosure, or treat every boundary as armor.
