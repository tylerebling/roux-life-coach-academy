REALISTIC COACHING SCENARIO

Alicia asks for answers while her language reveals a deeper conflict

Alicia, age 39, is deciding whether to accept a leadership role. She repeatedly asks the coach what to do, speaks quickly, minimizes her exhaustion, and becomes animated when describing meaningful work. Her coach uses presence, concise reflections, silence, permission-based challenge, and a sequence of questions that helps Alicia hear her own values and make a client-owned next step.
