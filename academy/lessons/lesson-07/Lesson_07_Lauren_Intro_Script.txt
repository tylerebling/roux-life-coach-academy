Welcome to Lesson Seven: Shame, Guilt, and the Inner Critic.

This lesson teaches a distinction that protects both dignity and accountability. A person can acknowledge harmful or ineffective behavior without turning one action into a verdict on her entire worth.

You will learn to recognize shame language, map the inner critic, support self-compassion without minimizing impact, and help clients move toward repair and responsible action. You will also learn when persistent shame, trauma symptoms, self-harm concerns, or severe impairment require licensed support.
