REALISTIC COACHING SCENARIO

One mistake has become Maya's entire identity

Maya is a respected small-business owner who missed an important client deadline during a difficult family week. She has apologized and offered a remedy, yet her inner language has become absolute: I ruin everything; I am not built for leadership. Your role is to help her separate behavior from identity, remain accountable, examine the critic's strategy, repair what is repairable, and choose a responsible next action.

Listen forObservable facts, identity verdicts, responsibility, repair already attempted, feared judgment, critic strategy, values, evidence, and current functioning.
Do notDiagnose trauma, argue that Maya did nothing wrong, use reassurance as the only intervention, demand disclosure, or substitute coaching for clinical support.
