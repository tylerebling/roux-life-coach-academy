Elena, age 42, accepted a leadership promotion and relocated with her family. The change is positive on paper, yet she misses her community, doubts her competence, and feels guilty for grieving. Her coach helps her name gains and losses, distinguish uncertainty from danger, reconnect with values, build support, and run small experiments without treating normal transition as pathology.

Listen forChanged facts, visible and invisible losses, emotional range, identity disruption, uncertainty stories, values, available support, capacity, agency, and clinical or safety concerns.
Do notRush gratitude, force closure, diagnose grief, promise a timeline, interpret all distress as resistance, treat trauma or depression, or decide whether the client stays, leaves, resigns, or relocates.
