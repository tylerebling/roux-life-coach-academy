ROUX LIFE ACADEMY
Lesson 2: Reflection and Practical Application Assignments

ASSIGNMENT 1 — A COMPLETE ROUX MAP
Choose a manageable, nonclinical coaching concern. Separate observable facts, the client’s story, and emotional information. Define a useful session goal. Write two Open questions and two Uncover questions. Design a possible Execute step while remembering that the client—not the coach—must choose it.

ASSIGNMENT 2 — TEN-MINUTE PRACTICE
Practice with a peer. The coach may not give advice. Spend approximately two minutes in each ROUX stage and two minutes reviewing the process. The observer listens for client ownership, permission, nonjudgment, and whether action connects to insight.

ASSIGNMENT 3 — COACH SELF-OBSERVATION
After practice, write where you felt tempted to advise, rescue, interpret, rush, fill silence, or work harder than the client. Choose one observable behavior to practice next time.

ASSIGNMENT 4 — SCOPE LANGUAGE
Write and rehearse a calm referral statement. Example: “What you are describing deserves the right kind of support. This extends beyond what coaching should address, so I recommend connecting with an appropriately licensed professional.”

SCOPE REMINDER
These exercises do not authorize diagnosis, trauma treatment, psychotherapy, crisis intervention, medical advice, or legal advice. Follow applicable emergency procedures when immediate safety concerns are present.
