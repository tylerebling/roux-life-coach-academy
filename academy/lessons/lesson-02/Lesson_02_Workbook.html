Roux Life Academy · Lesson 2

The ROUX Coaching Method

Guided workbook for Recognize, Open, Uncover, and Execute
Scope reminder: Use a manageable coaching issue. Do not use this exercise to diagnose or treat trauma, mental illness, immediate safety risk, or another concern requiring a licensed professional.

1. Presenting concern

Describe a realistic, nonclinical coaching issue in two or three sentences.

2. Recognize · Observable facts

What could a camera, calendar, email, or neutral observer confirm?

3. Recognize · Meaning and story

What meaning is the client assigning to those facts?

4. Recognize · Emotional information

What feelings are present, using the client’s own language?

5. Recognize · Session goal

What would make this conversation useful today?

6. Open · Two spacious questions

Write two questions that invite exploration without directing the answer.

7. Uncover · What matters beneath the surface

Identify possible values, needs, fears, beliefs, protections, or competing commitments.

8. Execute · Client-owned action

Design one specific, realistic next step the client could choose.

9. Prepare for interference

What could get in the way, and what is the smallest viable response?

10. Accountability agreement

How and when will the client review what happened without shame?

11. Scope check

What would tell you this concern needs clinical, medical, legal, or emergency support?

Coach’s closing reflection

Where were you tempted to advise, interpret, rush, rescue, or work harder than the client?
