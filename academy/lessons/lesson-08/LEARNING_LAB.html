REALISTIC COACHING SCENARIO

Tasha is trying to recover by pretending the setback did not matter

Tasha invested months in a community program that launched poorly and disappointed a close collaborator. She alternates between relentless self-criticism and forced positivity. Your role is to help her acknowledge the loss, regulate the pace, identify responsibility, attempt appropriate repair, learn from evidence, and build a resilient next step without providing therapy.

Listen forFacts, loss, needs, self-talk, responsibility, repair, available support, controllable action, current functioning, and evidence of recovery.
Do notRush gratitude, minimize grief, guarantee reconciliation, prescribe treatment, diagnose trauma, or make the coach responsible for Tasha's recovery.
TryWhat needs kindness, what needs accountability, and what needs time?
