Welcome to Lesson Eight: Self-Compassion, Repair, and Resilience.

Recovery does not require pretending that a setback was harmless. Self-compassion helps a client face reality without adding contempt. Repair addresses what can be addressed. Resilience is the capacity to adapt, recover, learn, and reconnect—not the ability to remain untouched.

This lesson will help you coach those processes while preserving client ownership and recognizing when licensed support is needed.
