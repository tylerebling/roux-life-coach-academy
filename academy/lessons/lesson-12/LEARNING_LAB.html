REALISTIC COACHING SCENARIO

Maya has postponed the same conversation for six months

Maya, age 37, carries most household planning while her partner assumes everything is fine. She alternates between silence and sharp comments, then feels guilty. Her coach helps her separate facts from story, regulate before speaking, make a specific request, prepare for several responses, and define her own next step without controlling the outcome.

Listen forObservable events, emotional meaning, unmet need, contribution, preferred outcome, request, consent, power, safety, and client-owned action.
Do notDiagnose either partner, decide who is right, script manipulation, encourage unsafe confrontation, treat trauma, promise reconciliation, or replace couples therapy or legal guidance.
TryWhat is the cleanest true sentence you can say about what happened, what matters, and what you are requesting now?
