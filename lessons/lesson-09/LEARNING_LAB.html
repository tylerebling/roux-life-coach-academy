REALISTIC COACHING SCENARIO

Maya experiences silence as proof that she is being abandoned

Maya, age 37, has not heard from her partner since yesterday. She sent six messages, checked social media repeatedly, and drafted a breakup text. Your role is to help her regulate, distinguish facts from conclusions, map the protective cycle, clarify relationship standards, and choose one dignified action without diagnosing either person or promising an outcome.

Listen forObservable events, body signals, the client's story, protective urges, short-term relief, longer-term cost, real relationship patterns, needs, standards, support, choice, and safety.
Do notDiagnose attachment styles, reassure without evidence, blame an absent partner, make the relationship decision, minimize emotional abandonment, or treat trauma symptoms.
TryBefore we decide what the silence means, what is happening in your body and what do you know for certain?
