REALISTIC COACHING SCENARIO

Elena keeps explaining, but no longer feels heard

Elena, age 42, says every conversation with her sister becomes an argument. She prepares long explanations, her sister becomes defensive, and Elena leaves feeling emotionally abandoned. The coach helps Elena slow the cycle, identify the need beneath her position, make one clear request, and choose a boundary she owns.

Listen forObservable interaction, emotion, meaning, need, protective strategy, the other person's autonomy, the client's standard, repair attempts, and what remains within the client's control.
Do notDiagnose either person, decide who is right, script a perfect outcome, push disclosure, minimize repeated dismissal, or treat trauma and clinical symptoms.
TryBefore we improve the wording, what do you most want your sister to understand, and what would being heard look like in observable behavior?
