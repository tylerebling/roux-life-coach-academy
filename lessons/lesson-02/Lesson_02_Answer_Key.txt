ROUX LIFE ACADEMY
Lesson 2 Final Assessment — Answer Key and Explanations

Passing standard: 80%. A learner must answer at least 10 of 12 questions correctly.

1. What is the primary purpose of the ROUX Coaching Method?
Correct answer: B. To provide flexible structure while preserving client ownership
Explanation: ROUX gives the coach a reliable path without replacing the client’s voice, pace, values, or responsibility.

2. Which statement is an observation appropriate for Recognize?
Correct answer: C. You became quieter when you described the promotion.
Explanation: An observation names what could be noticed without assigning an unconfirmed cause or diagnosis.

3. A client says, “I just hate conflict.” Which response best demonstrates Open?
Correct answer: B. What happens inside you when disagreement begins?
Explanation: This question creates spacious, nonjudgmental exploration before advice, action, or interpretation.

4. Elena stops working whenever her new business becomes visible. Which question best supports Uncover?
Correct answer: C. What becomes possible if the business succeeds—and what becomes uncomfortable?
Explanation: The question explores desire and protection together without shaming Elena or reducing the pattern to scheduling.

5. Which set of qualities belongs to a strong Execute step?
Correct answer: B. Specific, realistic, meaningful, client-owned, and connected to insight
Explanation: A useful action is clear enough to review, realistic in the client’s life, meaningful, owned by the client, and connected to learning.

6. A client did not complete an agreed action. What is the strongest accountability response?
Correct answer: C. What happened, what can we learn, and what needs to change in the plan?
Explanation: Accountability without shame treats the missed action as information and keeps responsibility with the client.

7. How is a framework different from a script?
Correct answer: A. A framework helps the coach understand what the conversation may need; a script prescribes exact words.
Explanation: A framework supports responsive judgment. A script can pull attention away from the client and toward prepared words.

8. A client says, “My manager excluded me, so she no longer respects me.” What should the coach clarify?
Correct answer: B. The observable event, the meaning assigned to it, and the emotional response
Explanation: Recognize becomes precise when the coach distinguishes what occurred, what the client believes it means, and what the client feels.

9. Which situation requires ordinary coaching to pause for appropriate professional or emergency support?
Correct answer: C. A client reports immediate intent to harm themselves.
Explanation: Immediate safety risk is outside ordinary coaching. The coach should respond calmly and follow appropriate emergency and referral procedures.

10. A client says, “Just tell me what to do.” Which response best protects ownership?
Correct answer: B. Before I offer any perspective, what feels most important for you to protect in this decision?
Explanation: The response acknowledges the request while returning attention to the client’s values and decision-making.

11. A new fear appears while the client is designing an action step. What should the coach do?
Correct answer: B. Return flexibly to Recognize or Uncover before continuing.
Explanation: ROUX is a loop, not a rigid sequence. New information may require the conversation to return to an earlier stage.

12. Which example shows appropriate professional vulnerability from a coach?
Correct answer: B. Saying, “I want to slow down because I may be making an assumption.”
Explanation: Appropriate vulnerability is brief, truthful, boundaried, and offered in service of the client—not the coach’s emotional needs.
