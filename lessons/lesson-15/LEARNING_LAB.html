REALISTIC COACHING SCENARIO

Danielle has a powerful goal—and a plan that cannot survive her real life

Danielle, age 36, wants to launch a consulting practice while working full time and caring for two children. Her original plan assumes fifteen extra hours every week and treats exhaustion as a motivation problem. Her coach helps her clarify the value, establish a baseline, reduce scope, sequence milestones, protect capacity, plan for obstacles, and create accountability without shame.

Listen forClient ownership, meaningful outcome, values, baseline, capacity, constraints, milestones, dependencies, obstacles, support, measures, review, functioning, and referral needs.
Do notChoose the goal, promise results, prescribe burnout, shame missed actions, ignore structural barriers, provide financial or legal advice, or treat clinical impairment.
TryWhat is the smallest version of this goal that would still honor the value and fit the life you are actually living?
