REALISTIC COACHING SCENARIO

Elena wants to be heard without becoming someone she is not

Elena is a respected operations director who has been quietly absorbing an unsustainable workload. She wants to ask her leader for clearer priorities and additional support, but she fears being labeled difficult, incapable, or ungrateful. Your role is to help her define the courageous act, prepare without scripting every response, preserve boundaries, and evaluate the result through values and evidence.

Listen forThe meaningful value, feared outcome, controllable action, support needs, power dynamics, safety considerations, and evidence after the conversation.
Do notTell Elena to confront her leader, guarantee a positive response, minimize workplace consequences, rehearse a manipulative speech, or treat distress outside coaching scope.
TryWhat is the smallest clear request that would honor both your limits and the work that matters?

REFLECTION + PRACTICAL APPLICATION

Practice before you coach

Personal reflectionIdentify one place where comfort and integrity are pulling in different directions. Name the value at stake.
Language rehearsalWrite an observation, impact, value or need, and specific request. Read it aloud until it sounds like you.
Partner practiceCoach one manageable courage decision for fifteen minutes. Track ownership, pace, pressure, and preparation.
Evidence reviewAfter the action, separate what you controlled, what occurred, what you learned, and what you will adjust.
