Welcome to Lesson Six: Practicing Courage in Real Life.

Courage is not a personality type, a dramatic speech, or the absence of fear. It is the practice of taking a values-aligned action while uncertainty is still present.

In this lesson, you will learn how to help clients choose proportionate courage, prepare for difficult conversations, ask for support, tolerate imperfect responses, and learn from real evidence. You will also learn when courage language must give way to safety, referral, and professional boundaries.
