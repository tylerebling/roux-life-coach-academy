Welcome to Lesson Five: Vulnerability and Emotional Armor.

In this lesson, you will learn to recognize the strategies clients use to protect themselves from uncertainty, disappointment, and being seen. We will treat those strategies with respect while examining their present cost.

Vulnerability in coaching is not oversharing. It is honest, relevant, appropriately protected action in service of values and connection. Your role is to support courage without forcing exposure, confusing coaching with therapy, or making the client dependent on your reassurance.
