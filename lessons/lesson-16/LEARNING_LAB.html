REALISTIC COACHING SCENARIO

Monica keeps restarting—and calling every interruption failure

Monica, age 42, wants a steadier morning routine after a demanding promotion and a family caregiving transition. She creates ambitious plans, follows them briefly, then abandons them after one difficult day. Her coach helps her map the loop, lower friction, regulate before choosing, build minimum and standard versions, plan recovery, and measure return rather than perfection.

Listen forDesired change, cue, context, emotion, nervous-system activation, automatic response, immediate reward, long-term cost, capacity, environment, identity story, support, functioning, and referral needs.
Do notDiagnose, shame, prescribe treatment, promise permanent change, treat exhaustion as laziness, ignore structural barriers, or make the client dependent on the coach.
TryWhat would make returning after disruption feel like part of the habit rather than evidence that you failed?
