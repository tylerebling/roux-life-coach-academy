Frequently Asked Questions

Is this certification accredited?+

Roux Life is a private professional coach-training certification built around practical, ethical coaching skills. It is not a state license, university degree, or mental-health credential.

How long do I have access?+

Your program access is designed for flexible, self-paced learning so you can revisit lessons and resources.

Do I need prior experience?+

No prior coaching experience is required. The curriculum builds from foundational skills through professional application.

What makes RLC different?+

It combines emotional courage, ethical coaching, attachment-informed pattern education, practical frameworks, and client-owned action.

How do I earn the certificate?+

Complete all lesson requirements and pass the comprehensive final exam with a score of at least 80%.

How do I return to my course?+

Select Continue learning or Sign in anywhere on this page to enter the protected learner academy.
