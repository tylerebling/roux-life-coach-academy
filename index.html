What this is

A complete life coaching course — not a weekend workshop.

Most coach training leaves you inspired but unequipped. Roux Life is built the other way around: forty hours of structured instruction that hand you an actual method — a way to run a session, hold a client accountable, and know exactly where your scope ends.

The curriculum

Six modules. One earned certificate.

Every module is a competency, and every competency is a line on your certificate. Read down — and watch it fill in.

  1. 01

    Foundations of Life Coaching

    What coaching is, what it deliberately is not, and the ethical boundaries that protect both you and your client.

  2. 02

    The Coaching Conversation

    Deep listening, questions that open rather than lead, and the session structure that turns talk into momentum.

  3. 03

    Goals, Values & Vision

    Help clients name what they actually want, then translate it into commitments they keep after the call ends.

  4. 04

    Working Through Resistance

    Recognize the patterns that stall progress — and move a client past them without pushing them away.

  5. 05

    Ethics, Scope & Referral

    Know your limits precisely. Document responsibly. Refer with confidence when a client needs more than coaching.

  6. 06

    Building Your Practice

    Packages, pricing, your first ten clients, and the simple systems that keep a coaching business running.

Questions

Before you enroll

Do I need any background to take this course?
No. The course starts at the foundations and assumes no prior coaching or counseling experience. Students arrive from every field — the ones who do best are simply the ones who finish the exercises.
How long does it take to complete?
The program is 40 hours of instruction and practice. Because it's self-paced, most students spread it across several weeks. There's no deadline and no cohort to keep up with.
Is life coaching the same as therapy?
No, and the distinction matters. Coaching is forward-looking and goal-oriented; it does not diagnose or treat mental health conditions. An entire module is devoted to scope, ethics, and knowing exactly when to refer a client to a licensed professional.
What do I receive at the end?
A Roux Life Certified™ certificate of completion for the 40-hour distance learning program, awarded once you've passed the competency assessments, along with the credential mark to use on your own site and materials.
Is the course fully online?
Yes. Every module is delivered online and works on a laptop, tablet, or phone. There is nothing to attend in person and no fixed class times.
Can I start a coaching practice right after?
That's what the final module is for. You'll leave with packages, pricing, and a plan for finding your first clients. Life coaching is an unregulated profession in most places, so no state license is required — but you should confirm the business requirements where you live.
